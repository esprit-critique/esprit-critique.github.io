Personal attack

Ad hominem, Argumentum ad hominem, Attaque personnelle

Rejecting an argument by attacking the person who makes it (their character, interests or past) rather than the argument itself.

The essentials

Argument diagram

A claims P.

A has a flaw.

So P is false.

Why the argument is invalid

The validity of an argument does not depend on who puts it forward. A dishonest or incompetent person can be right on a particular point. The attack is relevant only when personal credibility is the very subject of the debate, for example with a witness’s testimony.

In practice

Example

“That researcher criticises pesticides? He votes Green, so his study is worthless.”

Defending yourself

How to respond

Bring the discussion back to the argument: “Granted. What is wrong with the reasoning itself?”

Don’t confuse

≠ Not to be confused with: You too

“You too” is a particular form of personal attack: it targets the contradiction between what the person says and what they do.

Going further

Source

Analysed as early as Aristotle (Sophistical Refutations). Schopenhauer makes it the last stratagem of “The Art of Being Right” (published 1864): when you have no arguments left, become personal.
