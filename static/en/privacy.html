Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

In short: without sync, no data leaves your browser. If you turn on sync, an encrypted copy of your progress is kept by Cloudflare under an identifier derived from your code: this is pseudonymised data, and therefore personal data under the GDPR (details below). No cookies, audience tracking, advertising or external resources (fonts, scripts or images from other sites). If you turn on sync, a copy encrypted on your device is kept by a host; no one but you can read it, not even the publisher.

What stays in your browser

Your progress is saved in your browser’s local storage (“localStorage”): entries marked as learned, quiz answers and reviews, workshop results and levels, forecasts and decisions you record, anything you have spotted, cards read, paths followed, mastery, rank, clarity points and distinctions, daily goal, nickname (optional) and settings (theme, animations, plain mode). Without sync, this data never leaves your device; the publisher never has access to it. It is used only to provide the features you ask for: this strictly necessary storage does not require consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act), which is why there is no banner.

The site also keeps, in your browser, up to four copies of this progress: one per day, and one just before an import or a restoration. They let you go back from Your data.

To let you read the site offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the site’s files (a service worker). It contains no data about you.

Your rights over this data

You exercise them from the site, with no account: the publisher can neither read your online copy nor find it without your code.

  • Access and portability: “Save to a file”, in Your data.
  • Deleting the online copy: “Delete the online copy”, in Profile, Settings (immediate deletion on the server).
  • Erase it: “Erase my progress”, in the same place (the automatic copies are erased too), or by clearing the site’s browsing data.
  • The files the site creates (backup, .ics calendar) are produced on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Sync (optional)

If you turn it on (Profile, Settings), your progress is encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM) before being sent. For each copy, the server keeps: a 32-character identifier derived from your code, the fingerprint (SHA-256) of a token also derived from your code, the encrypted content (256 KB at most), a version number, and the dates of creation and last write. Neither your code, nor your nickname, nor your IP address is recorded in this database. No email address, name or password is collected; the site cannot recover a lost code. The code is kept on your signed-in devices so that it can be shown again.

This is pseudonymised data: no one can read the content without your code, but it remains personal data under the GDPR.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Sync is only turned on if you ask for it; you withdraw your consent by deleting the online copy.
  • Where: in a Cloudflare D1 database created with the “European Union” jurisdiction (storage in the Union).
  • How long: as long as you use it; a daily purge deletes any copy left 24 months without a write.
  • Processor: Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers service and D1 database), under its data processing agreement. Like any host, Cloudflare sees the IP address of requests, which the site’s server does not record.
  • Transfer outside the Union: Cloudflare is an American company, and requests may be handled by its data centres outside the European Union. These transfers are covered by Cloudflare’s certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and, failing that, by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914).

The host

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub receives requests for pages: according to its documentation, visitors’ IP addresses are recorded and kept for security reasons. The publisher has no access to these logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Sharing

The “Share” button opens your device’s sharing menu, or copies the link: the site itself sends nothing. The “Share this card” and “Share my rank” images are drawn on your device, then handed to the sharing menu or saved to your files: they go through no server.

Installing the site as an app (home screen) sends nothing either: your device keeps the page and its files.

Contact and complaints

For any question: the repository’s issues (a GitHub account is required; your rights, however, are exercised from the site, with no account). You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).
