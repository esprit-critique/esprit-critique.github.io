Bias blind spot

Angle mort des biais

Readily recognising cognitive biases in others while believing oneself less influenced.

The essentials

Mechanism

We judge others by their behaviour, but ourselves by introspection. Yet biases are not accessible to introspection: perceiving nothing biased in oneself does not prove that one is not.

In practice

Example

After reading a list of cognitive biases, many people mostly recognise their acquaintances in it.

Defending yourself

How to protect yourself

Rely on procedures rather than on your own impression of objectivity: criteria fixed in advance, blind evaluation, review by a third party.

Don’t confuse

≠ Not to be confused with: Dunning-Kruger effect

The bias blind spot concerns awareness of one’s own biases; the Dunning-Kruger effect, the assessment of one’s competence in a field.

Going further

Source

Pronin, Lin and Ross (2002). Participants rate themselves, on average, as less prone to biases than the average American and than their peers.
